**StreetsEmpire 1.3: Law Enforcement, Wanted Level & Corruption.**

Heat has always been the noise: it rises fast, cools in hours and decides whether one more trip gets
you busted. Now every city's police also keep a **Case** on you, the memory of what you have done there.

## Your Case

- **One Case per city, and only you can see it.** It builds from part of the Heat you draw in a city
  and from what the police see: busts, arrests, road stops, torches, sacks, hijacks and big cash
  movements. Nothing on a profile, a scout report or a public result shows it, and no other player
  can add to it.
- **A receipt for every change.** The Case panel on your dashboard lists exactly why your Case is
  what it is.
- **The Wanted ladder:** Quiet, Noticed, Under Investigation, Warrant, Federal. Nothing on it is
  rolled, and every rise tells you on the dashboard, in Activity and, if you want, on Discord and
  your phone. At Under Investigation the panel tells you what the detectives are looking at.
- **It cools.** Stay quiet in a city for a while and its Case goes cold. Laundering washes it too.
- **It resets.** Every Case starts at zero each season.

## Warrants

At the Warrant stage, a warrant names one target, your Hideout, a business or you personally, and
gives you a warning window before it is served. In that window you can move what you can, lawyer
up to turn it into a fine, or have a District Attorney quash it. Or take it: a served warrant closes
most of the Case.

- Raids honour your Safe Room, never take a block, and police losses are capped each day.
- A lawyer on retainer softens every raid and arrest.

## Corruption and informants

- **A payroll of officials, city by city.**
  - A Precinct Captain warns you earlier and gives you longer to respond.
  - A District Attorney slows a Case and can quash a warrant.
  - A Judge softens raids and downtime.
  - A Customs Officer eases airport checks.
- **Every favour leaves a trail.** Past a line, Internal Affairs opens a file and warns you. Cut the
  official loose, or risk the sting.
- **Informants sell information, never protection:** word on the federal sweep, or how a city's
  police work.

## Cities and the Feds

- **Every city's police work their own way.** Beverly Hills keeps files warm, Atlanta looks the other
  way, and Las Vegas lets things slide until it doesn't.
- **A local Case stays behind when you move house.** A Case at Federal follows you, and the move
  screen tells you before you confirm.

## Ledger, and a clean record

- **Ledger,** a retired records sergeant, has seven one-time Jobs about how the law works, with law
  titles and a Case File profile frame. Like every Job reward, they never pay cash, turns or
  protection.
- **Three clean-record feats,** judged when a season ends: Clean Record, Nothing on Paper and Off the
  Books.

## What doesn't change

Heat, busts, arrests and bribes work exactly as before. Play the Heat game sensibly and you will
rarely, if ever, see a warrant. Ignore it, and expect one about once a week.
