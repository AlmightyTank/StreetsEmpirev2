**StreetsEmpire 1.0 is here, and Season One is open.**

StreetsEmpire is a free browser game about running a crew in a city that does not want you there. Seasons last a few weeks. At the end the standings freeze, the winners go into the Hall of Fame for good, and everyone starts the next season level.

## What a season is

- **Your crew.** Your crew works the streets, cooks product, and stocks up at the corner store, at Pip's and at the traders.
- **Turns.** Every move costs turns, and turns refill on their own clock.
- **Heat.** Push too hard and the police notice.

## Everything in 1.0

- **Crew and streets.** Scout the five kinds of district. Every product changes how your workers do the job.
- **Trade.** Pip's counters and a high market in each of eight cities, with real supply and prices that move. Stores sell weapons, Low-Riders and supplies, and traders open up as your reputation grows.
- **Fights.**
  - Recon before you commit, then raid, drive-by or pull a special move.
  - Wounded thugs heal on a clock, or faster with medicine.
  - Every fight gets a full report, and a hit on you opens a revenge window.
- **Runs.** Load Low-Riders with cash and cargo and drive to another city to trade. You can be tailed and hit on the way, and your crew or your alliance can send backup.
- **Turf.**
  - Take blocks from the locals, post thugs to hold them, and push rivals off theirs.
  - Set up outposts away from home.
  - An alliance that holds enough of a city controls it.
- **Your Hideout.**
  - Five rooms: Safe Room, Lookouts, Workshop, Back Office and Garage.
  - Upgrades change how you play, and specializations are yours for the season.
- **Alliances.** Invite your crew, share recon, post on the alliance wire, fight side by side and climb the alliance rankings.
- **Quests and favours.** Jobs from the people who run the city, with follow-ups that open as you go and seasonal events.
- **Playing together.**
  - Private messages and a contacts book.
  - Alerts in the bell, on your phone, and on Discord if you link it.
  - Blocking, muting and reporting when someone crosses the line.
- **History.** Final standings, the Hall of Fame, the public season archive, and your own career across seasons.

## New for launch

- **A real first hour.** A short intro and a getting-started guide, and every page explains itself.
- **Fair play, enforced.**
  - The server refuses exploits, catches linked accounts and flags anything strange for staff.
  - Staff have proper tools to pause a season, void a fight or ban a cheater, and every staff action is recorded.
- **Phones first.**
  - Every page works at phone width, and the buttons are big enough to hit.
  - Install it to your home screen like an app, and turn on phone alerts.
  - It tells you when a new version is out.
- **It stays up.** Monitoring, daily backups that are restored and checked every week, and a tested recovery plan.

## The rules, briefly

One account each, no bots, no using bugs, and be decent. The full [terms](https://streetsempire.dev/terms) and [privacy](https://streetsempire.dev/privacy) pages are on the site.

See you on the streets.
