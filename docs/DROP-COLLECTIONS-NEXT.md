# The Drop: next 12 collections (brainstorm)

Status: brainstorm, not built. Launch catalogue and rules: [THE-DROP.md](THE-DROP.md).

## Constraints every set follows

- **Only items that have art today** can be dressed: Sedan, Low-Rider, Van,
  Pistol, Shotgun, Tek-9, AK-47, Thug, Hoe. Each look is one partial-coverage
  style key, like the launch sets.
- **No overlap** with looks sold or earned elsewhere: Street Pass S1 (Fire/Ice
  Dragon, Chrome Serpent, Siren Breaker, Phantom Convoy, Lantern District,
  Gilded House, Laurel Ascendant, Dead or Alive, Snowstorm, Inferno, Block
  Sovereign), supporter and earned looks (Cartel Gold, Midnight Ops, Urban
  Ghost, Midnight Chrome, Obsidian Signal, 8-Bit), the hidden easter eggs
  (Holiday Road, Blue Sky, Time Machine) and the launch Drop palettes
  (black/brass, violet/teal neon, frost/snow).
- **Same SKU shape** as launch: vehicle, crew (two outfits or one set),
  optional weapon finish, frame, accent, shell with an illustrated scene,
  title, and one bundle at about 60% of list. Every animation stops under
  reduced motion.
- **Same price bands**: vehicle 800–850, crew outfit 700 (set 900), weapon
  550–600, frame 450, accent 300–350, shell 1,200–1,250, title 200–250.

### 1. Graveyard Shift (seasonal, Halloween)

Moonlit cemetery work: bone, moss and a cold ghost-green glow.

- **Sedan:** black hearse conversion, wrought-iron roof rail, lace curtains in the rear glass.
- **Thug:** undertaker's frock coat, bowler, bone-handled cane. **Hoe:** black lace mourning dress and veil.
- **Pistol:** bone-inlay grip, etched skull-and-vine slide.
- **Frame:** a wrought-iron gate ring with mist drifting through the bars.
- **Shell:** cemetery gates, crooked trees, a full moon and low fog rolling across the page.

### 2. Naughty List (seasonal, December)

The stash house at Christmas: wrapping paper, foil and string lights. Not snow (Winter Heist owns that).

- **Van:** candy-stripe delivery van with a giant bow on the roof and a "Naughty" plate.
- **Crew set:** ugly-sweater hoodie with a chain (Thug); red velvet coat with white trim (Hoe).
- **Tek-9:** gift-wrap finish with a ribbon-tied grip.
- **Frame:** a ribbon ring tied off with a bow; the bow wobbles once on hover.
- **Shell:** rooftops in string lights, wrapped boxes stacked by a back door, a sack of coal.

### 3. Heartbreaker (seasonal, Valentine's)

Love gone cold: crimson satin, roses and thorns.

- **Low-Rider:** candy-apple red, a chain heart steering wheel, rose-pattern hydraulics.
- **Thug:** rose-embroidered black bomber. **Hoe:** red satin dress and heart-shaped shades.
- **Tek-9:** rose-chrome with an engraved thorn vine.
- **Frame:** a thorned rose ring with a single falling petal.
- **Shell:** a rainy street under a flickering broken-heart sign, petals in the gutter.

### 4. Block Party (seasonal, summer)

Hot day, open hydrant, music in the street.

- **Van:** an ice-cream truck with a speaker stack on the roof.
- **Thug:** track jacket and bucket hat. **Hoe:** bright two-piece, big hoops.
- **Shotgun:** sticker-bombed finish.
- **Frame:** a speaker-cone ring that thumps gently to a beat.
- **Shell:** brownstones, string flags, an open hydrant spraying, a DJ table on the stoop.

### 5. Biscayne Sunset

1980s waterfront money: pastels, white linen, deco hotels.

- **Sedan:** white convertible look with a pink pinstripe and teal interior.
- **Thug:** white linen suit over a pastel tee. **Hoe:** teal sequin dress with a palm-print wrap.
- **Pistol:** pearl grip, polished slide.
- **Frame:** a pink and teal deco ring.
- **Shell:** deco hotel fronts, swaying palms and a sunset gradient over the water.

### 6. Velvet Speakeasy

Prohibition nightlife: oxblood velvet, cream and pewter (not brass, Kingpin Luxe owns that).

- **Sedan:** vintage running-board sedan with whitewall tyres.
- **Thug:** pinstripe three-piece and fedora. **Hoe:** beaded flapper dress and feather headband.
- **AK-47:** walnut furniture and a drum magazine.
- **Frame:** a velvet-rope ring on pewter posts.
- **Shell:** a hidden bar behind a sliding bookcase, a gramophone and drifting cigar smoke.

### 7. Platinum Funk

1970s street royalty: burnt orange, chocolate brown, platinum and fur.

- **Low-Rider:** long-hood cruiser with a fur dash, whitewalls and a hood ornament.
- **Thug:** wide-lapel suit and platform shoes. **Hoe:** fur coat over a disco jumpsuit.
- **Frame:** a disco-ball ring that scatters slow light flecks.
- **Shell:** a roller-disco floor with sunburst stripes and turning lights.

### 8. Redline Syndicate

Underground street racing: carbon fibre, racing red and white livery.

- **Sedan:** widebody kit, a rear wing and race-number livery.
- **Thug:** patched racing jacket. **Hoe:** pit-crew jumpsuit and headset.
- **AK-47:** carbon furniture with red trim.
- **Frame:** a tachometer ring whose needle sweeps to the redline.
- **Shell:** a parking-garage meet with light trails and tyre smoke.

### 9. Wet Paint

Train-yard graffiti: bubble letters, chrome fills and paint drips.

- **Low-Rider:** a full mural paint job with bubble-letter tags.
- **Thug:** paint-splattered hoodie and respirator. **Hoe:** tagged denim jacket.
- **Pistol:** drip finish.
- **Frame:** a ring that drips spray paint.
- **Shell:** a yard wall covered in tags, with a fresh piece slowly appearing.

### 10. Hazard Cook

The cookhouse in hazmat gear: warning yellow, stainless steel and black hazard stripes. It must not borrow Blue Sky's blue glass.

- **Van:** a lab RV with a vent pipe and drums strapped to the back.
- **Crew set:** hazmat suits with respirators for both types.
- **Shotgun:** stainless with hazard-stripe tape.
- **Frame:** a hazard-stripe ring.
- **Shell:** a desert lab at dusk, bubbling flasks and a flickering work lamp.

### 11. Dockside

The harbour after dark: navy, rust and safety orange.

- **Van:** a box truck in shipping-container livery.
- **Thug:** peacoat and watch cap. **Hoe:** longshore work jacket and boots.
- **AK-47:** a weathered, salt-pitted finish.
- **Frame:** an anchor-chain ring with rope lashing.
- **Shell:** container cranes, harbour lights in the fog and a slow searchlight sweep.

### 12. Crown Jewels (flagship)

A set named for the currency: emerald, ruby and sapphire on platinum and
velvet. Make it the most expensive set and a natural Featured slot.

- **Sedan:** an armoured state car with a jewelled grille (1,000).
- **Crew set:** an ermine-collar coat (Thug) and a jewelled gown (Hoe) (1,000).
- **AK-47:** platinum with jewel inlays.
- **Frame:** a crown ring with jewels that sparkle in turn.
- **Shell:** a vault of jewels and a throne room seen through the vault door.

## Build checklist per collection

Following the launch sets (see THE-DROP.md):

1. `packages/rulesets/src/drop.ts`: collection, items and bundle.
2. `packages/shared/src/cosmetics.ts`: `ITEM_COSMETIC_STYLES`, outfit text and `COLLECTION_COVERAGE`; the style key list in `packages/shared/src/schemas/auth.ts`.
3. Art: add generators to `scripts/art/generate-drop-art.mjs`, write SVGs to `apps/web/public/items/cosmetics/drop/`, register them in `apps/web/src/items/itemCosmeticArt.ts`.
4. Frame and accent CSS in `apps/web/src/styles/drop-art.css`; shell tokens in `drop-shells.css`.
5. Shell scene in `apps/web/src/components/theme-scenes/`, registered in `SiteThemeDecor.tsx`.
6. Tests: the key and style collision test, coverage, and the profile and easter-egg key lists.
7. Seasonal sets: admin catalog windows and return policy.
