# 0.4.0-E full-round product simulation

Ruleset: classic-og-v0.4-e. 28 days of Scout trips, 12 turns each, as expected values.
Trips cycle through Casino District 30%, Nightclub District 25%, Urban Ghetto 15%, Low Rent District 15%, Wino Slums 15%.
Whore happiness is held at 85, girls are covered, the cut is 50%. Product is bought at Pip's price within his shelves; whatever
a shelf cannot supply falls back to crack, then runs dry. Heat rises, cools and busts as in play. Combat, cooking and bribes are left out.
Score is final cash plus whores at their net worth value.

## Gate

**Passes.** In every play style and start, a strategy that mixes products by district beats every single-product strategy.

## Twice a day

A banked cap of 144 turns, spent at once, morning and evening. Shelves and Heat reset between sessions.
