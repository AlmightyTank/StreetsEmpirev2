# Account XP pacing · classic-og-v2.2-a

28-day seasons, played back to back. 136 one-time Jobs and 9 season quests a round. XP: 1/turn for the first 300 turns a day, half after (regen 2 per 5 min, 144 to start), daily 75, weekly 200, city/event/alliance 150, Job 100, season 300. Double XP on days 19, 20, 21 of each season. Check-ins 25/day, Roll Call 300, intro 150 once, season finish by final rank (Hardcore #8, Active #40, Casual #200, Weekends only #400).
