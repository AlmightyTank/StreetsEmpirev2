# Career Storylines 2.4.0 — Reward and Moderation Contract

Career story rewards are recognition, not a second economy. Every branch in
catalog `2.4.0-A` is budgeted as one cosmetic/profile moment and zero cash,
inventory, combat, turf, or permanent competitive power.

## Allowed rewards

- Cosmetic title or badge keys already handled by profile/legacy systems.
- A profile moment whose provenance names the career, chapter, branch, and
  catalog version.
- Contact/faction relationship references only when the existing relationship
  service accepts the verified source.

## Forbidden rewards

- Cash, product, weapons, crew, turns, turf, private intel, or combat power.
- Rewards based on another player's private state.
- Duplicate grants from repeated activity delivery.

Every persisted portfolio update carries `catalogVersion`, chapter/branch
information, and the stable source receipt where applicable. Moderation copy
must describe earned recognition as cosmetic/history-only and must not imply a
competitive entitlement.
