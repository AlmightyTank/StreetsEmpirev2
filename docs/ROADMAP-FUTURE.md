# StreetsEmpire — Future Development Roadmap

## Post-1.0 Expansion Plan

This roadmap intentionally begins **after StreetsEmpire 1.0**.

Versions are suggested themes rather than commitments. Features can move between releases based on player feedback and actual season data.

The guiding rule after launch should be:

> Expand the world without invalidating the systems players already understand.

---

## 1.1 — Businesses, Fronts & Rackets

### Theme

Move from controlling street corners to controlling legitimate-looking operations.

### Possible businesses

- Nightclub
- Bar
- Strip Club
- Chop Shop
- Pawn Shop
- Auto Garage
- Convenience Store
- Warehouse
- Casino Front
- Laundromat

### Core loop

Acquire → operate → upgrade → protect → profit.

Businesses could provide:

- Legal-looking income
- Product distribution
- Storage
- Crew jobs
- Logistics
- Information
- Limited laundering

### Rackets

Certain businesses can support criminal rackets.

Examples:

**Nightclub**
- Ecstasy demand
- Higher nightlife income
- Information network

**Chop Shop**
- Stolen Low-Riders
- Vehicle parts
- Vehicle recovery

**Warehouse**
- Product storage
- Run loading
- Shipment capacity

### Guardrail

Businesses should require active strategic choices rather than becoming passive infinite-income buildings.

---

## 1.2 — Casino & Gambling Expansion

### Theme

Make casinos actual destinations rather than just district flavor.

### Games

Potential games:

- Slots
- Blackjack
- Poker
- Roulette
- Dice

### Casino cities

Cities such as Las Vegas can gain stronger gambling identity.

### Player risk

Casino losses permanently leave the player's seasonal economy.

Casino wins should remain mathematically controlled.

### Future possibilities

- Casino tournaments
- High-roller tables
- Limited daily jackpots
- Casino achievements
- Seasonal gambling statistics

### Guardrail

Gambling never uses real-money purchases.

---

## 1.3 — Law Enforcement, Wanted Level & Corruption

### Theme

Expand Heat into a deeper city-wide risk system.

### Possible systems

- Wanted levels
- Police attention
- Investigations
- Raids on Hideouts
- Warrants
- Informants
- Bribery
- Corrupt officials
- Evidence pressure

Different crimes could create different attention:

- Street work
- Product sales
- Turf violence
- Convoy hijacking
- Large cash movement

### Player choice

A player could:

- Stay quiet
- Bribe officials
- Move cities
- Reduce visible operations
- Accept higher risk for higher profit

### Guardrail

Law enforcement should create strategic pressure, not random unavoidable punishment.

---

## 1.4 — Contracts, Factions & Underworld NPCs

### Theme

Make each city's underworld feel populated even outside player competition.

### NPC organizations

Possible groups:

- Street gangs
- Biker clubs
- Cartels
- Mafias
- Corrupt officials
- Smuggling crews
- Local fixers

### Contracts

Examples:

- Move a package
- Protect a shipment
- Hit an NPC crew
- Recover a vehicle
- Collect a debt
- Supply a product order
- Hold a block
- Deliver weapons

### Reputation

Separate faction reputation could unlock:

- Contracts
- Prices
- Information
- Unique products
- Cosmetic titles

### Guardrail

NPC content supplements PvP instead of becoming the safest optimal money source.

---

## 1.5 — Vehicles & Garage 2.0

### Theme

Expand Low-Riders into an actual fleet system.

### Possible vehicle classes

- Low-Rider
- Sedan
- SUV
- Van
- Box Truck
- Armored Car
- Performance Car

Vehicles could differ in:

- Cargo
- Escort capacity
- Speed
- Heat
- Visibility
- Durability
- Convoy defense

### Garage systems

Potential additions:

- Repair
- Vehicle storage
- Upgrades
- Loadout presets
- Insurance
- Specialized run vehicles

### Guardrail

Do not turn vehicles into dozens of meaningless stat variations.

Each type needs a distinct strategic job.

---

## 1.6 — Property & City Expansion

### Theme

Give wealthy crews more meaningful ways to establish themselves across the map.

### Property ideas

- Safe houses
- Warehouses
- Apartments
- Garages
- Business properties

Properties may support:

- Storage
- Outposts
- Travel logistics
- Local intelligence
- Temporary recovery

### New cities

Only add cities when they have a reason to exist.

A new city should introduce differences such as:

- Product demand
- Store availability
- Road position
- Police pressure
- Local turf difficulty
- Unique businesses
- Casino access

Never add cities that are simply another copy of New York.

---

## 1.7 — Crew Identity & Management

### Theme

Make the empire feel more personal without turning crew members into hundreds of individual RPG characters.

### Crew roles

Thugs could be assigned in groups to:

- Street security
- Turf
- Convoys
- Hideout
- Businesses
- Protection

### Specialists

Limited named specialists could include:

- Enforcer
- Driver
- Cook
- Lookout
- Accountant
- Mechanic

These should be rare strategic modifiers, not a full character-management simulator.

### Crew history

Profiles might record:

- Peak organization size
- Famous victories
- Longest-held turf
- Historic business empire

---

## 1.8 — Competitive Events & World Events

### Theme

Give seasons occasional disruptions and objectives.

### Examples

- Police crackdown
- Product shortage
- Major trader shipment
- City festival
- Turf bonus week
- National heat wave
- Port closure
- Economic boom
- Underworld contract event

Events should affect all players under transparent rules.

### Competitive events

Potential limited competitions:

- Most profitable run
- Most turf captured
- Largest product shipment
- City-control contest

Rewards should favor cosmetics, trophies and recognition over permanent competitive power.

---

## 1.9 — Community & Supporter Features

### Theme

Give players ways to support StreetsEmpire without turning payment into competitive advantage.

### Membership ideas

Possible supporter benefits:

- Profile cosmetics
- Extra profile themes
- Supporter badge
- Additional statistics views
- More saved UI presets
- Cosmetic crew banner
- Additional notification customization
- Forum cosmetics

### Never sell

Do not sell:

- Turns
- Cash
- Product
- Weapons
- Combat strength
- Turf bonuses
- Faster production
- Exclusive profitable markets

The competitive season should remain fair.

---

## Longer-Term 2.0 Direction — National Empire

2.0 should only exist if the 1.x game proves that players want a much larger strategic layer.

### Possible vision

Move from:

> Build the biggest crew in a city.

to:

> Build an organization with influence across the country.

Possible systems:

- Larger organizations above alliances
- Regional territory
- Organized interstate supply chains
- Multiple business holdings
- Large-scale faction politics
- National rankings
- Organization wars
- National contracts
- Expanded world map

### Critical requirement

2.0 must build on existing StreetsEmpire gameplay rather than replace it.

Scout, Product, Combat, Travel, Turf, Hideout and Stores should still matter.

## Development Philosophy After 1.0

Each major update should answer one question.

**1.1:** What do I own besides turf?

**1.2:** What can I do for entertainment and risk?

**1.3:** What happens when the law notices me?

**1.4:** Who else runs these streets besides players?

**1.5:** What do I drive and move my empire with?

**1.6:** Where does my organization physically exist?

**1.7:** Who actually runs my operation?

**1.8:** What changes from one week or season to another?

**1.9:** How can the community support the game fairly?

**2.0:** What happens when my empire becomes bigger than one city?
