# 0.2.0-G street polish

0.2.0-G keeps the F combat balance and starts the player-facing polish pass. The goal is to make the public raid round easier to understand without exposing private combat intel for free.

## Status

- Ruleset: `classic-og-v0.2-g` at version `0.2.0-G`.
- Combat model version: `0.2.0-G.1`.
- Default seed: `Game #007 - Street Polish`.
- Balance: inherited from 0.2.0-F.

## First G slice

- Special raid reports now explain the actual outcome in player language:
  - drug runs say how many hoes were hit and how many supplies burned;
  - ride theft says whether a Low-Rider came home or stayed put;
  - lure runs say how many people crossed over or why nobody moved.
- Target options now mark scouted crews directly in the dropdown, and the selected target line shows when fresh intel expires.
- The game nav now includes a Community link for `forum.streetsempire.dev`.
- The free forum direction is Flarum first, with Discord login early and Street Empire SSO later.
- The homepage, news wire, health check and seed path now identify the active work as 0.2.0-G.

- Add a safe forum URL setting instead of hardcoding the forum domain.
- Give recon a richer target card so the player sees public info, known intel and blocked reasons in one place.
