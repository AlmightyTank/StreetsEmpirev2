# Quest System — Phase H Side Jobs

Phase H expands the unified Jobs & Contacts system with optional side work for the three contacts introduced earliest in the story: Mama King, Pip and Tommy.

It does not add permanent unlocks, favors, timed buffs, repeatable contracts or new quest-only actions. Those remain later roadmap phases.

## Goals

- make contact reputation unlock more work
- add variety without duplicating gameplay systems
- give players optional jobs alongside the main story chain
- keep every objective driven by existing Scout, Produce, Store and Combat events
- preserve the original ten-job story catalog in older pinned rulesets

## Mama King — Street Operations

### Recruitment Drive
Unlock: complete `Fresh Faces`.

- recruit 10 hoes through Scout
- bonus: recruit 5 thugs
- reward: $7,500, 50 condoms, +10 Mama rep

### Night Shift
Unlock: complete `Keeping Them Happy` and reach 30 Mama rep.

- spend 24 Scout turns in the Nightclub
- earn $15,000 from Nightclub Scout trips
- bonus: recruit 3 hoes there
- reward: $10,000, 25 beer, +15 Mama rep

### House Full
Unlock: complete `Payday` and reach 50 Mama rep.

State requirements:

- 25 hoes
- 15 thugs
- 150 condoms
- 50 beer
- 75 crack
- bonus: 10 armed, fit thugs

Reward: $12,500, 15 medicine, +20 Mama rep.

## Pip — Products & Distribution

### Bulk Order
Unlock: complete `Cookhouse`.

- produce 150 Meth
- bonus: produce 50 Crack
- reward: $10,000, +15 Pip rep

### Party Favors
Unlock: complete `Cookhouse` and reach 25 Pip rep.

- produce 100 Ecstasy
- bonus: sell 50 Ecstasy to Pip
- reward: $12,500, 50 crack, +15 Pip rep

### Move the Weight
Unlock: complete `Cookhouse` and reach 40 Pip rep.

Sell to Pip after accepting:

- 100 Weed
- 50 Ecstasy
- 50 Meth

Reward: $20,000 and +20 Pip rep.

## Tommy — Weapons & Muscle

### Stock the Crew
Unlock: complete `Heavy Hands`.

- buy 15 pistols from Tommy
- bonus: have 15 armed, fit thugs
- reward: $7,500, 5 medicine, +10 Tommy rep

### Two Collections
Unlock: complete `Eyes Open` and reach 30 Tommy rep.

- win 2 raids
- bonus: run recon twice
- reward: $15,000, 10 medicine, +15 Tommy rep

### Patch Job
Unlock: complete `Collection Day` and reach 50 Tommy rep.

- treat 10 wounded thugs
- bonus: have 20 armed, fit thugs after treatment
- reward: $12,500, 25 medicine, +20 Tommy rep

## Catalog compatibility

Phase H is layered into `classic-og-v0.7-f`, the current beta ruleset.

The pinned catalogs remain:

- 0.7-D: 10 story jobs
- 0.7-E: 10 story jobs
- 0.7-F: 10 story + 9 side jobs = 19 total

This prevents historical 0.7-D/E rounds from silently gaining content they were not created with.

## UI

Quest cards now identify Story versus Side job.

Locked quests remain excluded from the Available tab. They still exist in player state so prerequisites and progression remain durable, but the Available view only shows work the player can actually accept.

## Phase boundary

Phase H uses only existing reward kinds:

- cash
- items
- contact reputation

Permanent weapon/product unlock expansion remains Phase I.
