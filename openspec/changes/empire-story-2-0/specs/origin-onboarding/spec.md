# Spec Delta

## Purpose

Teaches StreetsEmpire by playing it: a short origin story that delivers a contact, a business win, a dramatic encounter, and a career choice in 15 minutes.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Four-beat origin replaces checklist for new players

New players SHALL get a 4-beat chain (meet contact, first business opportunity, dramatic encounter, career choice) driven by existing quests and pending encounters.

#### Scenario: Complete in one sitting
- **WHEN** a new player starts
- **THEN** all four beats are reachable within 15 minutes without opening every mechanic page

### Requirement: Origin grants no head start

Origin rewards SHALL be fixed introductory amounts identical for all new players and SHALL NOT exceed normal early-game earnings.

#### Scenario: Equal start
- **WHEN** two players finish different origin branches
- **THEN** their starting resources differ only cosmetically
